Hebrew Calendar AI Companion for eisy. It connects your smart home to the Jewish calendar so you (or your AI assistant) always know what's happening on any given day, and can plan automations around it.

It can:
- Tell you what Jewish holidays, Shabbat, and other observances fall within a date range, along with candle lighting and havdalah times for your location
- Convert any regular calendar date to its Hebrew calendar equivalent, and vice versa
- Find every future occurrence of a recurring Hebrew date, such as a birthday or a yahrzeit (memorial anniversary), across a range of years
- Show today's Hebrew date on its status node
- Feed all of the above to your eisy AI assistant, so you can simply ask things like "when does Shabbat start this week" or "what date is Rosh Hashanah next year," or ask it to help schedule lights and thermostats around candle lighting and havdalah

## Parameters

None. This plugin works out of the box using your eisy's configured location and time zone to calculate accurate local times.
